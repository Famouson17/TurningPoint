Who We Are

Rooted in scripture. Raised for the church. Sent to a continent.

Turning Point Theological Seminary is a Christ-centred institution dedicated to biblical scholarship, theological education, spiritual formation, ministry preparation and Christian leadership.

Originally founded as Turning Point Bible Institute, TPTS pursues a clear vision — to become a leading centre of biblical, theological and ministerial education, equipping Christ-centred leaders for transformational service in Africa and the wider world.
